Problem

Improve campaign efficiency and identify creators worth re-investing in across multiple product categories.

Strategy

Combine daily performance monitoring with a reusable pool of high-performing creators.

Action

Managed major campaigns with individual budgets of 300,000–3,000,000 (currency not specified in the CV). Tracked CPM, CTR, CPE and ROI; coordinated creator placements on Xiaohongshu, Douyin and Bilibili. Built a creator re-investment library adopted by the business.

Wrote an operations SOP adopted by the team and built Coze workflows for content discovery and competitor monitoring.
